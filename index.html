How to install on your unit
    
      
  1. Extract it on your computer. Keep the ZIP and recovery/ safely off the unit.
    2. 
      
  2. Stop playback and mute monitoring. Disconnect USB, power off, then hold GRF6 while powering on. Release it and connect USB.
    3. 
      
  3. Confirm the AIRAMODULAR drive and VERSION.TXT. With no conflicting update/ROM files, copy only the two files inside install/ to the drive root. Do not copy the ZIP, both folders, or extra firmware files.
    4. 
      
  4. Safely eject. If Windows says “in use,” close files and retry; do not force-eject. Disconnect USB only after successful eject.
    5. 
      
  5. When the center is light blue and GRF6 flashes, press GRF6 once. Keep power on throughout. If the indicators differ, stop and seek help.
    6. 
      
  6. Expected completion on the tested unit: blue center and both GRF buttons flashing together. Then power-cycle normally and verify personality. Lights alone are not proof of a correct flash.
    7. 
      
  7. Initialize the selected model in Customizer as needed. Test at low volume. A new full-ROM backup allows byte comparison with the package.
    8. 
    
The recovery package restores this input snapshot, including its saved patches. It cannot guarantee recovery if the bootloader is damaged.

  

  
Compatibility & making a backup
Conversion currently requires the validated boot/updater code, build-0493 application fingerprint, native ROMINFO range, and one unambiguous Product record. It does not require one owner’s complete ROM hash. Unknown builds and ambiguous record histories are inspection-only. Build 0491 is recognized but not enabled for conversion.
Evidence: four personalities and a return to Scooper tested on one Scooper. A matching layout on another unit is not independent hardware validation.
Hidden full-ROM backup gesture observed on that unit: disconnect USB; hold GRF6 at power-on, release; press GRF5 ten times before connecting USB. Copy both files from BACKUP/ to your computer. This gesture is not established for every firmware. Never substitute factory reset.
