How to make a backup — start here
  1. Stop playback, mute monitoring, disconnect USB, and turn the unit off.
  2. Hold GRF 6 while switching power on, then release it.
  3. Press GRF 5 ten times before connecting USB.
  4. Connect USB. Open the AIRAMODULAR drive, then its BACKUP folder.
  5. Copy AIRA_MODULAR_ROM.BIN and ROMINFO.TXT to a safe folder on your computer. Keep this original pair untouched.
  6. Safely eject the drive and disconnect USB. Restart normally when finished. Select your saved copies below.

This undocumented backup sequence was tested on our Scooper running 0493. Build 0491 has the same updater code, but the sequence has not been hardware-verified on 0491. If BACKUP does not appear, stop and ask for help. Do not copy update files or start a restore just to make a backup.

v1.05: 0493 tested on one Scooper; 0491 experimental, offline-tested only.

An official *_UPD.BIN update or USERAREA.BIN is not a full-ROM backup.

Compatibility details
Requires known boot/updater code, an exact build-0491 or build-0493 application fingerprint, native ROMINFO range, and one unambiguous Product record. Unknown builds and ambiguous record histories are blocked.
0493: four personalities and a return to Scooper tested on one Scooper. 0491: shared updater and Product getter/setter code checked offline; package tests use a synthetic in-memory fixture, not a captured 0491 backup. No 0491 hardware restore is verified. Other units are not independently validated. The tool preserves your firmware version; it does not upgrade 0491 to 0493 or fix its bugs.
